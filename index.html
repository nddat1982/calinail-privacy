Cali Nail Pass - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Cali Nail Pass is a study app for the California manicurist exam. It is an independent, community-made app and is not affiliated with the California Board of Barbering and Cosmetology or PSI.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your study progress (answers, scores, saved terms, streaks and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local daily study reminders on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Text-to-speech

The app may use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read terms aloud. This is handled by your device's operating system.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional licensing exam.
